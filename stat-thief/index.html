About Stat Thief

Stat Thief turns Premier League history into a drafting game. Instead of picking a squad, you build a single fictional legend one attribute at a time. You spin through real clubs and real players and steal one statistic from each name that comes up. After ten rounds you are left with a composite footballer who never existed, and then you find out what kind of career that player has.

How to play

Every run starts with a position. Forward, Midfielder and Defender each weigh the eight attributes differently, and the reels only surface players who fit the role you picked.

Each round spins two reels: first a club, then a player from that club. When the player lands you see eight attributes graded from S to F, a playstyle trait and a nationality. You keep exactly one. Every option shows how much it would move your overall rating right now, so you can see when a B+ in a heavy attribute beats an S in a light one.

You get one club respin and one player respin per draft. Random events are announced before you spin: some boost or weaken the next player, others hand you an extra respin. Three steals from the same club, or a nationality backed by other steals from that country, earn a bond bonus on top of your rating.

After ten rounds your legend gets an overall rating and a tier. Then the career starts: pick from three contract offers, play the big game of the season, finish the league, chase the Champions League and the Ballon d'Or, and spend your wages on training and recovery as your player ages. Retire whenever you like after 30, and the best careers enter your Hall of Fame.

Frequently asked questions

Where do the stats come from?

Each player's numbers are loosely inspired by the qualities they are remembered for, simplified into an S-to-F grade so drafts stay fast and readable. They are meant to feel familiar to football fans, not to serve as accurate performance data.

Is Stat Thief affiliated with the Premier League or any club?

No. Stat Thief is an independent, unofficial fan project. Club names, colours and player references are used for identification and entertainment only, and Stat Thief has no affiliation with any league, club or player.

What is the daily draft?

Once a day everyone gets the same draft: the same position, the same clubs, players and events. Your choices are the only difference. Your best daily score is kept on your device.

Does progress save between visits?

Your best draft and your Hall of Fame of finished careers are stored in your own browser's local storage. Nothing is sent to a server. A draft or career in progress lasts as long as the tab is open.
