Stat Thief guides

Everything the game does not stop to explain: the maths behind the overall rating, what each grade is worth, and how experienced players spend their respins.

Five guides · updated September 2026

A Stat Thief run takes eight minutes, which is not long enough to teach you the systems underneath it. These guides do that. They are written from the actual rules of the game rather than from guesswork, so the weightings, thresholds and bonuses quoted here are the ones the code really uses.

Where to start

If you have never played, read How to play and then just draft something - the systems make much more sense once you have watched a build come together badly. If you already have a few runs behind you and you are stuck somewhere in the high seventies, go straight to Draft strategy; that is where the ceiling usually is.
